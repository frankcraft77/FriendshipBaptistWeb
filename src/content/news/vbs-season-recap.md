Every summer, sanctuaries and fellowship halls across Blount County fill up
with crafts, snacks, songs, and squirming kids — it's VBS season, and it's
one of the best examples of our churches at work.

Behind the scenes, the association helps churches get ready: training for
directors and teachers in the spring, shared decorations and supplies
passed from church to church, and volunteers who serve at more than one
VBS as the summer rolls on.

A post like this could share photos, thank volunteers, and report how many
children heard the gospel across all our churches combined — a story no
single church could tell alone.
