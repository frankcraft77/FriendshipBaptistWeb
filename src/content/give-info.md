Generosity is at the heart of how Baptist churches work together. Our
member churches support the association's ministries through their regular
giving, and every gift multiplies: it strengthens local churches, cares for
pastors, funds county-wide outreach, and — through the **Cooperative
Program** — supports thousands of missionaries and ministries across
Alabama, North America, and the world.

**The simplest way to give today** is through your local church — every
one of our member churches would be glad to help you give to their
ministry and to our shared work.

Online giving directly to the association is being prayerfully considered.
When a decision is made, this page will be updated with a simple, secure
way to give.
