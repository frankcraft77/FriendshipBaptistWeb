We're glad to introduce the new online home of the Friendship Baptist
Association. Our hope is simple: that this site helps people across Blount
County find a church family, and helps our member churches stay connected
to one another.

A few things to explore:

- **[Find a Church](/find-a-church/)** — type your address and see the
  churches nearest you, or browse the whole directory.
- **Church pages** — every member church has its own page with service
  times, directions, and contact information.
- **[Events](/events/)** — associational gatherings, trainings, and
  special services, all in one calendar.

This site will keep growing as churches add photos and details to their
pages. If you spot something that needs updating about your church, let
the association office know — it only takes a minute to fix.
