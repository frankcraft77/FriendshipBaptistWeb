Southern Baptists have always believed that churches can do more together
than apart. Through the Cooperative Program and associational missions,
even the smallest congregation in our association has a hand in ministry
that circles the globe — supporting missionaries, planting churches,
training pastors, and responding to disasters.

But cooperation isn't only about what happens far away. Right here in
Blount County, our churches team up for food drives, school outreach,
community events, and neighbor-helping-neighbor care that no single church
could sustain alone. When you serve with one of our churches, you're part
of all of it.
